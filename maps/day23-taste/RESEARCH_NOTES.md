# Research notes and exclusions

## Scope and branch decisions

Only historic Old Dhaka is included. Similar names are not treated as proof of a
shared business. Nanna uses the Becharam Deuri location documented by an owner
interview; Beauty uses the original Johnson Road shop. Mirpur Kolkata Kacchi,
Jurain Beauty Boarding and newer branches outside Old Dhaka are excluded. Royal
is identified in Lalbagh. Duplicate OSM points for Nanna, Nirob, Royal and Beauty
Boarding are consolidated into one establishment each, without moving coordinates.

## Khichuri evidence

- **Shomsher Ali’r:** bhuna khichuri, documented at Bangshal/Sat Rawza. Do not infer
  every meat variant from a generic khichuri mention.
- **Star, Johnson Road:** the branch-specific menu lists chicken and mutton bhuna
  khichuri. Chicken is indexed under Morog Khichuri, with that normalization noted.
- **Manik Chan:** the latest cited 2025 report lists mutton bhuna khichuri on Thursdays.
  Its 2023 chicken schedule differs; the newer report takes precedence.
- **Aftab:** khichuri in a 2012 report. Exact meat/style is not inferred; current
  operation is uncertain.
- **Beef Khichuri:** researched, but not assigned to a mapped restaurant on inadequate
  branch/coordinate evidence. It is deliberately absent from the dish selector.

## Quality limits

These are curated discoveries, not a restaurant census or a live business directory.
No current founding-date claims, prices, hours or food photos are invented. Aftab
and Haji Makhan are clearly marked as current-operation uncertain. Check current
service locally, especially outdoor stalls and seasonal dishes. Dense storefront
clusters retain their real coordinates; zoom or the keyboard-accessible place
selector can resolve overlapping markers without artificial location jitter.
