      ONE TRIP. EVERY MILE COUNTS.

      How far can 1,000 miles take you?

      1. Click a highway to choose your starting point.
      2. Add stops to build a real highway route.
      3. Every distinct state crossed by the route counts.
      4. Your trip must stay within 1,000 highway miles.
      5. Collect more states to beat your personal best.

      Drag numbered pins, reorder or delete stops in Your Stops, or undo an edit. Over-budget changes are rejected. A starting pin alone counts zero states until you drive a routed segment.

      Mileage uses this game’s simplified major-highway network, not real-time driving directions. DC does not count. Your personal best stays in this browser.
