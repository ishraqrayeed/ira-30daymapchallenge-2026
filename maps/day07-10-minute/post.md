30DayMapChallenge 2026 | Day 7 — Accessibility

Same city. Quite the journey.

Denver Union Station and Denver International Airport are both within Denver city limits, yet they're separated by a 25-mile drive.

Ever wondered why Denver's city boundary stretches so strangely toward the northeast? Back in 1988, Denver annexed roughly 53 square miles of land from Adams County to build its new airport, which opened in 1995. That explains the unusual shape you see on the map.

For Day 7, I kept things simple. Took about 10 minutes to put this together using OpenRouteService (ORS) to generate the driving route, Kepler.gl to visualize it, and PowerPoint for the final layout.
