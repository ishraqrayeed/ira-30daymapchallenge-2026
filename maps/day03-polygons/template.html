Sources & modeling assumptions

Stations and operating service

Sixteen stations, Uttara North–Motijheel, in their mapped MRT Line 6 order. Kamalapur is excluded: the project director reported it still under construction on 17 October 2026. Coordinates come from OSM station nodes/building centroids and were cross-checked against citylines.co. Farmgate uses the mapped station-building centroid, not the center of the wider Farmgate neighborhood.

Estimated rail and walking time

Actual OSM rail alignment determines relative leg lengths. Running times are apportioned by those lengths, calibrated to the published approximately 38-minute all-stations journey, including an assumed 30-second dwell at each intermediate station. Individual legs are estimates. Time zero is at Farmgate station: travel to Farmgate, entry, ticketing/security and initial train waiting are excluded. No transfers or reboarding are modeled. Intermediate station dwells are included; no departure dwell at Farmgate or arrival dwell at the exit station is added. A 2-minute exit allowance and the mapped access-to-network distance are included. Walking speed is 4.8 km/h. The walk-only option from Farmgate includes the same exit allowance and station-to-street connector; no rail ride is required for that option.

Pedestrian network and polygons

OpenStreetMap/Geofabrik snapshot __SNAPSHOT__. Walkable streets and paths are routed by shortest travel time, respecting mapped topology and explicit private/no-foot restrictions. The largest connected walking network is used; most station access points fall back to the station center because entrance mapping is sparse. Partial reachable edges are interpolated at each time budget. A 60 m envelope around reachable links creates the catchment; mapped water is removed and disconnected slivers below 2,500 m² are dropped. Disconnected meaningful pieces remain. The underlying 15/30/45-minute geometries are checked for nesting. The display uses non-overlapping bands and solid sequential teal colors, so transparency does not mix them. Interior gaps smaller than 50,000 m² are filled for visual simplicity; major mapped water and larger access gaps remain. This cartographic generalization does not change analytical areas or routing. Areas use WGS 84 / UTM zone 46N (EPSG:32646).

Dhaka city outline and percentage

The single, gently smoothed geofence merges Dhaka North and Dhaka South City Corporations from the BBS / OCHA-HDX COD-AB v03 dataset, valid from May 2023 and reviewed in June 2025. It is not Dhaka District. The approximate precise underlying overlap is __CITY_PRECISE__%. The percentage divides the original 45-minute street-envelope intersection by the original __CITY_AREA__ km² municipal union, including its water and restricted land. It is an area-based model result, not population coverage or verified parcel access. Display smoothing is never used in that calculation.

What this approximation leaves out

No GTFS, timetable-specific departures, initial waiting, service disruptions, queues, congestion, individualized walking speeds or detailed stair/lift penalties. OSM can omit paths, crossings, entrances and access restrictions. The street envelope generalizes nearby urban blocks; it does not prove that every parcel inside is accessible, and may span small unmapped barriers. Its 60 m width is a polygon-generation assumption, not extra walking reach. GeoJSON areas use analytical polygons; display edges are simplified by at most 10 m.
