# Every Line a Climb

[Open the map](map.html)

158 mapped trail segments; approximately 314.7 miles of selected linework inside the NPS boundary. Sampled elevations span 7,780–14,248 feet.

Only segments at least 0.5 miles long are shown. Segments are dataset pieces, not complete hikes. Distances describe linework; no route completion, current access, or difficulty is inferred. [Sources and methods](README.md).
