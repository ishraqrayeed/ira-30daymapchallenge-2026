# What Messi Saw — Day 05 / Sight

35 source-recorded Messi shots across eight 2026 World Cup matches, including
8 goals and 2 match penalties. No shootout attempts. Geometry summaries use the
33 non-penalty attempts: median 18.7 m and 19.4°. Hero views remain 47.9°
(widest), 9.8° (narrowest, off target), and 16.9° (longest goal, 24.6 m).

Source: WhoScored / Noah Bair; counts cross-checked against FIFA reports compiled
by Hesham El Alamy. Metric coordinates use marking-aligned normalization to a
105 × 68 m pitch with 7.32 m goal width. This represents geometric goal opening,
not literal vision. No xG or trajectories are used.

The approved 1080 × 1350 portrait fits the visible browser area. Its 35 markers,
70 rays, 3 annotations, centred goal, and analytical data were checked. Six
geometry/data tests pass; phone-scale and normal-zoom composition were reviewed.
Separate raw event downloads are not provided. The event repository has no
explicit open-data license; attribution is retained without claiming one.
