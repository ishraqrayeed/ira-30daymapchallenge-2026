Day 1 uses fatal crashes involving pedestrians in Denver, 2021–2025. Later days
will explore mobility, street networks, and spatial analysis. Digital outputs are
interactive HTML; social images are browser screenshots. Day 16 requires a real
collaborator, and Day 30 is drawn by hand.
