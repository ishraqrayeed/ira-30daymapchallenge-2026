READING THE INVENTORY

What are historic ratings?

These are the Town of Telluride’s preservation classifications. They describe historic significance and the condition of historic character.

Contributing
Largely original or appropriately restored, and clearly contributes to the historic district.
Supporting
Altered, but still conveys a sense of history.
Local Landmark / Significant Landmark Interior
Specifically recognized landmark classifications in the Town’s inventory.

The brick-red group combines these four ratings and excludes records marked demolished or “old structures map.” It contains 293 polygon footprints. Some building identifiers repeat or are missing, so this is not a count of distinct buildings.

A rating is not a construction date. The inventory does not support a complete pre-1900 filter. Streets and footprint geometry are contemporary.
