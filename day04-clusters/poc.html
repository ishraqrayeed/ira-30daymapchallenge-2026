Data, evidence & limitations

Include food establishments with explicit OSM diet:halal=yes/only or a business name explicitly containing halal. Cuisine alone never qualifies. Halal in a name is evidence of a public claim, not independent certification. Original source coordinates are used; no geocoding. NYC inspection observations are first collapsed by business ID, then sources are deduplicated by normalized name/address and proximity. Every included location passes a point-in-polygon test against Manhattan.

Coverage depends on volunteer mapping and registered business names. Carts are particularly easy to miss or mislocate. Businesses open, close or move, and halal status can change. This map cannot establish where all halal food is concentrated, compare neighborhoods fairly, or verify certification. DBSCAN describes this sample; no significance or random-baseline test is claimed.
